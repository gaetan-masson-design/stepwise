# Style profile: Organic melodic

This is the target sound for Stepwise's first user: folk-rooted songwriting turned into warm, hypnotic dance music, with live guitar, a low sung voice, and analogue synths over an organic groove. It's a style description built from genre conventions; it isn't based on any one artist's songs.

**How settled each point is:**
- **Established:** a widely documented convention.
- **Working assumption:** good enough to build on.
- **Open:** not settled yet.

## Character
- **Key:** mostly minor; A minor and E minor are typical. *(established)*
- **Tempo:** about 120–124 BPM for club tracks (122 is a good default); 80–105 for downtempo and ballads. *(established)*
- **Chords:** simple 3–4-chord loops built from open guitar shapes, reused across verse and refrain. Only a bridge moves away. *(established)*
- **Colour moves:**
  - A major IV inside a minor loop: a Dorian lift.
  - A major V just before home: a harmonic-minor ending.
  *(established)*
- **One-chord vamps** over a moving bass also fit. *(working assumption)*

## How songs get written in this style
1. **Write a song on guitar or piano first.** *(established)*
2. **Loop the idea** and jam layers over it (synth, guitar, bass). *(established)*
3. **Find the melody by singing** over the loop, often in nonsense syllables at first. *(established)*
4. **Arrange late,** and lightly: over-arranging kills the idea. *(established)*

## Melody and voice
- **Low to mid range, speech-like, narrow, built from short repeated phrases.** *(working assumption)*
- **Stacked vocal harmonies** on the hook. *(established)*

## Lyrics
- **Short:** about 150–220 words per song, with heavy line repetition. *(established)*
- **The hook is often 2–3 words,** repeated 3–4 times. The opening stanza often returns at the end. *(established)*
- **Imagery is elemental:** water, tides, fire, sun, desert, darkness. Themes are surrender, change, cycles and rebirth, and dreamlike storytelling. *(established)*

## Sound
- **Clean electric guitar through a long delay** is the centrepiece. *(established)*
- **Analogue synth stabs,** soft pads and arps. *(established)*
- **Groove:** a warm, round kick; organic percussion (shakers, hats panned around); a gliding mono bass; short plucks. *(established)*
- **Club versions** follow the standard intro → build → breakdown → drop blocks of 16/32 bars. *(working assumption)*

## Starting loops for this style
These come from the [harmony library](harmony-patterns.md):
- **Campfire:** i–VI–IV, a chord per bar, as a 3- or 4-bar loop.
- **Wanderer:** i–♭VII–iv twice, then ♭VI–V–i.
- **Homecoming:** ♭VI–♭VII–i for a refrain lift.
- **Hypnotic:** i only, over a moving bass.

## What this means for Stepwise
1. **"Organic" vibe preset:** 122 BPM, minor, a Campfire loop, delay-guitar pluck, soft pad, gliding bass.
2. **Plain-language colour toggles:** "Dorian lift" (iv becomes IV) and "Strong ending" (major V before home).
3. **Loop first, arrange later:** an arranger that turns the loop into sections.
4. **Lyric helper:** 8–16 lines, a 2–3-word hook repeated 3–4 times, elemental image prompts.
5. **Hum to melody:** sing over the loop and snap to the scale.
6. **Percussion-friendly grooves:** shakers and roaming hats, since the user is a percussionist.
